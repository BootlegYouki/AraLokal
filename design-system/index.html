Material Design 3 System

Design standards and UI components for L.A.R.A (DepEd Elementary Grades 1 to 6)

Elementary Design Principles

L.A.R.A is built for Filipino elementary pupils (Grades 1 to 6) on entry-level Android devices and school laptops. The UI enforces large tap targets, high-contrast text, clear iconography, and distraction-free layouts.

  • Touch Targets: Minimum 48dp on all clickable elements (preferred 56dp for primary actions) to prevent miss-taps by young children.
  • High Contrast & Clean Typography: Minimum 4.5:1 text-to-background contrast ratio for public school classroom lighting.
  • Bilingual Localization: Complete interface parity between English and Filipino.
  • No Hidden Menus: Essential classroom tasks (Stream, Classwork, Quizzes) are reachable in one tap via the bottom navigation bar.
